# No Reservaitions Quality Gate

Use this as a final pass before publishing any new No Reservaitions review.

## Required before drafting

- Resolve each user-mentioned dish or drink against the official menu or best corroborated source when possible.
- Capture canonical item names plus 2-5 notable components for resolved dishes.
- Mark unresolved items explicitly instead of flattening them into vague nouns.
- If a low-confidence item would materially weaken the review, ask Dan a follow-up question before drafting.

## Draft must do

- Read like a remembered experience, not an agent summary.
- Mention at least 2 concrete specifics that go beyond category-level nouns.
- Include at least 1 sentence explaining what a dish, drink, or ordering sequence meant in context.
- Carry judgment and appetite, not just recap.
- Fit the stop type: full meal, bar stop, breakfast, concert/venue, etc.

## Draft must not do

- Mention "the photo," "the image," "the picture," or otherwise narrate source artifacts.
- Leave composed dishes reduced to flat nouns like "watermelon" when menu research shows a fuller plate.
- End with generic consumer-advice formulas like "if you want X, this may not be your room."
- Reuse stock openings, stock closings, or obvious agent filler.
- Sound like a compliance report about evidence handling.

## Fast failure phrases

If any of these appear, stop and rewrite:

- "The photo tells you"
- "The image makes the case"
- "It looks like what it is"
- "The room is the star"
- "gave the order some lift"
- "sounds like a very solid move"
- "sounds like a perfectly solid call"
- "if you want X, this is/isn't your room"

## Publish threshold

Do not publish unless the draft feels closer to a sharp, articulate recollection of the night than to an AI summarizing notes.
