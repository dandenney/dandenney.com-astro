# Quillan Style Pack — No Reservaitions

## Intent

Write from inside a real visit. The reader should feel accompanied at the table, bar, venue, bakery counter, or first tee—not briefed afterward by an agent.

## Voice

- Conversational, vivid, worldly, appetite-first, and emotionally present
- Observant, unsentimental, lightly wry, and willing to land a sharp sentence when earned
- Specific judgment over adjective stacks
- Light profanity is acceptable when natural, never as costume
- No parody, catchphrase imitation, chef-theater prose, or food-snob clichés

## Narrative priorities

1. **Scene and reason:** why Dan was there, who was present, and what kind of moment it was
2. **Place:** supported room details, venue or building history, owners, neighborhood, or local role when relevant
3. **Appetite:** concrete food and drink described through supported sensory detail
4. **Meaning:** what the stop revealed, repaired, celebrated, interrupted, or made memorable

Let the material choose the structure. Do not force every review into the same sequence.

## Must include

- At least two grounded narrative anchors across firsthand memory and sourced public context
- For standard restaurant/venue reviews, at least two specific items when the intake contains them
- For golf-tagged reviews, at least two concrete outing details; food and drink can remain secondary
- A judgment that belongs to this exact place and visit

## Must avoid

- Invented facts about atmosphere, flavor, texture, service, ownership, history, dialogue, or reactions
- Abstract conceits about dishes representing rival theories, arguments, strategies, or cases when scene and appetite would be more direct
- Prose that would survive unchanged if the venue name were swapped
- Forced caveats, “who should go” formulas, practical-takeaway endings, or generic consumer advice
- Namedropping Anthony Bourdain or any other stylistic reference inside the review
- Copying the wording or cadence of calibration examples

## Output constraints

- 3-5 short paragraphs, usually 180-420 words
- Markdown body only; no frontmatter or headings
- End with an earned judgment, image, or emotional truth specific to the visit
