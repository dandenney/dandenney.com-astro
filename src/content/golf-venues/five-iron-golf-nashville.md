Five Iron is the first place in this log where a swing produced a number. Trackman bays, digital courses, and a screen that turns every strike into carry, ball speed, and a dispersion dot. For anyone wired to enjoy feedback loops, that alone makes the room interesting.

The catch is what the room is for. It is a bar with simulators, tuned for bouncing between digital courses with a pitcher on the table, and the pace pulls toward play rather than practice. There is no coach, no drill structure, and no reason to stop after a good one. As a place to get better, it is a mirror with no one standing behind it.

That is not a knock. It was a good night, and it set the appetite for what came two days later at GOLFTEC, where the same numbers show up with someone who can explain them. Five Iron stays on the list for a rainy night with people who want to play a digital Pebble, not for reps.
