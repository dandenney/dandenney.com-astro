Harpeth Valley is a driving range with a nine-hole par-3 course attached, which makes it the right first real course for two people who learned this game indoors. From the Reds it plays 1,088 yards for a par of 27, rated 26.8 with a 79 slope. No driver needed. Every hole is one swing to find the green and then a lot of decisions after that.

The greens were the story. They were fast and they were honest, and most of our strokes were putts. PopStroke had us feeling decent about the flat stick. Real slope on real grass on a real course is a different conversation.

We walked it with a pull cart on a beautiful fall day. Nerves had us playing faster than we needed to, but we each got some nice shots in, and on the seventh a 7 iron stopped six inches from the cup for a tap-in birdie. Dan shot 45, Misty shot 56, and the Vice Royal Gold made it all nine holes without getting lost.
