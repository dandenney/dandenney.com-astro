I spent Monday and Tuesday at [Imagine IF](https://www.imagineifnashville.com), put on by Bitcoin Park here in Nashville. The theme was AI, Energy, and Bitcoin, all pointed at freedom tech. That's a different crowd than my usual front-end and data conferences, and I was curious how those three would actually fit together.

Rod Roudi kicked things off by saying his goal was for us to be entertained, inspired, and informed. It delivered on all three. The format helped a lot: mostly 10 minute talks with some longer panels, all in the theater at the Fisher Center. It's a beautiful venue, and lunch was excellent with no lines and a great day for eating outside.

If I had to boil the two days down to one idea, it's this: we aren't in an arms race, we're in a capabilities race. Energy is an enabler for people, and it's what nations are truly fighting for right now. The future will belong to the people who deliver it, shared with the people who provide the compute.

The topics didn't line up much with my day-to-day, but they gave me a lot to think about as we keep teaching AI and machine learning at DataCamp.
