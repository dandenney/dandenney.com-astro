The groove is relaxed and a little understated, and that restraint is where the song lives.

The titles do some of the talking. A battery runs down quietly, and the album it sits on is called "I'm fine," the phrase people reach for when they'd rather not explain. As track 10 of 21, "Battery" takes that pairing and lets it sit. The melancholy lives in the texture: a breathy delivery, an easy groove, nothing that sounds like it's asking for help. It sounds like getting through the day on whatever charge is left, and not bringing it up.

The production is dry and polished, every part set apart with room around it, and the delay gives the guitar a trailing edge that hangs in all that space.
